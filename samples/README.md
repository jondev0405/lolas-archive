# Sample audio

`sample-memory.wav` is a short, synthetic test recording generated with the Windows
speech synthesizer. It is here so you can try the flow without using a real family
recording.

The sample says:

> Every Sunday, my lola made pandesal before the sun came up. She said, food is how we
> say I love you. She hummed Dahil Sa Iyo while the kettle boiled.

Use your own recording for a real memory. Put private audio in `uploads/` through the
app, not in this folder.
